How it works

Write, check, fix, send. In that order, every time.

01

Write

AI drafts each email from web research plus your projects or your offer. Or write one template and let merge fields fill it per contact.

02

Check

14 local checks: capitals, pressure words, link safety, leftover placeholders, length, calls to action, the subject line and the unsubscribe line.

03

Fix

Anything scoring 4 or more gets one targeted AI rewrite. If it's still risky, it waits in your review queue with the exact reasons.

04

Send

Delivered from your own Gmail with a random 1–3 minute gap between emails, retried on rate limits and never sent twice.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does a low score guarantee the inbox?

No tool can promise that. Inbox placement also depends on your domain's reputation and the recipient's filters. The guard catches the content problems you control, before they cost you.

Can Outreach Desk read my email?

No. It asks Google only for the gmail.send scope, which can send mail you approve but can't read, search or delete anything.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. Template campaigns with merge fields work end to end without an AI key. AI is used for personalization and for rewriting flagged drafts.

What happens to people who unsubscribe?

Add them to your suppression list once and they're skipped in every future campaign.

How does Premium get activated?

After you buy Premium we add it to the email on your account, usually within a day. It lasts 30 days; buy again to renew. Premium users never see the database itself, only the companies they've emailed.

What if the server restarts mid-campaign?

The campaign is marked interrupted, and you can resume it from the contact where it stopped.
