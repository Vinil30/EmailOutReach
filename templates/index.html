How a message moves through Outreach Desk

Four stages, every time — nothing sends without passing through the first three.

01

Draft

Give it the recipient and the context. It writes a first pass shaped for that role and company, not a template with blanks.

02

Analyze

Every draft runs through a local spam-risk guard before you see a send button. You get a score and the exact reasons behind it.

03

Correct

Flagged lines are marked with what triggered them, so you rewrite a phrase instead of starting over.

04

Send

Mail goes out through your own Gmail account over OAuth. Outreach Desk never sees your password.

analysis Local spam guard

Every draft gets a deterministic risk score before it ships, broken down by the signals that raised it — not just a pass or fail.

sending Gmail OAuth

You authorize once. Every message sends from your own inbox, under your name, so replies land where you already work.

reliability Retry state

Rate limits, timeouts, and server errors are tracked per message, so retrying never means sending the same email twice.
