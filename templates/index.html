    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Do the emails really come from my Gmail?

    Yes. You connect Gmail once, and every email is sent from your account, so it appears in your Sent folder and replies come straight to your inbox. Mailzen only asks for permission to send; it can't read your inbox.

    What's the difference between Pro and Premium?

    Both give unlimited sending (up to 100 a day, to keep your Gmail safe), one-click automation and recruiters from our database: 200 new ones per purchase on Pro, 500 on Premium. Pro sends ready-made templates; Premium adds AI that researches each company, picks your best project, writes a unique email and fixes anything the spam check flags. Each purchase lasts 30 days.

    How do Pro and Premium get activated?

    Buy using the email you signed up to Mailzen with. We activate it on that account, within 5 hours. Every purchase gives you a fresh batch of recruiters you haven't emailed before.

    Can I see or download the recruiter database?

    No. The database stays private; Mailzen sends to it on your behalf. You see each company and contact you've reached, and the full emails are in your Gmail Sent folder.

    What's the difference between a template and AI?

    A template sends the same email to everyone, with only the recipient's name and company changed and your featured project kept the same. AI-personalized (Premium) researches each company, picks your best-matching project and writes a unique email.

    What can I do on the free plan?

    Upload your own list of contacts, pick a template, and send up to 20 emails in total, all spam-checked and sent from your Gmail.

    Will this get my Gmail flagged?

    Mailzen sends one email at a time with a random 1–3 minute gap, never repeats a recipient, and blocks anything that looks like spam before it leaves. No tool can guarantee the inbox, but these are the habits that protect your account.
