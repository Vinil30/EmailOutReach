1. What Mailzen does for you

Mailzen helps you build a profile with your resume link, skills and projects; pick or write email templates; optionally have AI research each company and write a personal email; spam-check every email; and send from your own Gmail, by hand or through automation, while tracking which companies you have reached.

2. Honest outreach

  • Only describe your own education, skills, projects and experience, and keep them accurate. Don’t claim degrees, jobs, offers or referrals you don’t have.
  • Read AI-written emails before relying on them, and fix anything that is not true about you.
  • Use Mailzen only to ask about jobs, internships, referrals or advice, not to sell products, promote services or collect money.
  • Keep messages polite and professional.

3. Recruiter contacts

Some accounts can email recruiters from a database Mailzen maintains. Those contacts are made available only so Mailzen can send your individual outreach. You will not see the full list, and you must not:

  • copy, export, publish, sell or share any recruiter’s details you learn through Mailzen;
  • contact a recruiter outside Mailzen in a way that harasses them, or keep following up after they ask you to stop;
  • use the contacts for anything other than your own job search.

Mailzen does not promise that any contact is current, hiring or will reply. If a recruiter asks us to stop, we deactivate them in the database so automation never emails them again.

4. Your own lists

If you upload your own contacts, only include people you reasonably expect to welcome a job enquiry, such as recruiters, hiring managers or people who have published a professional address for this purpose. Don’t use bought or scraped lists.
