Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 8 November 2026Version 2026-11-08Questions: rapellysaivinil30@gmail.com
On this page 1. Credit packs 2. Plans (Pro and Premium) 3. How refunds are paid 4. Cancelling

This policy covers payments to Mailzen for plans (Pro and Premium) and for credit packs. It is part of our Terms. To ask for a refund, email us from your Mailzen account address with your payment ID.

The short version

  • Charged but didn't get your credits or plan, or charged twice? Full refund.
  • Unused credit packs can be refunded within 7 days if none of that pack was spent.
  • Credits not used by a purchase come back to your balance automatically.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5-7 working days of approval.

This summary is for convenience. The full text below is what applies.

1. Credit packs

  • Failed or duplicate payments: if you were charged but no credits arrived, or charged more than once for one purchase, we refund the extra in full.
  • Unused packs: within 7 days of buying, you can ask for a refund of a pack if none of its credits were spent. Once credits from a pack have been used, that pack can't be refunded.
  • Unused credits inside a purchase (for example, a web search that found fewer contacts than you asked for) are returned to your credit balance automatically. They are not paid back as money.
  • Bounced contacts: if a contact turns out not to exist, report it with "Mark as bounced" on the Sent page. We review reports and may add credits back to your balance.

2. Plans (Pro and Premium)

  • Plans last 30 days from activation and don't renew automatically, so there's nothing to cancel.
  • If your plan wasn't activated, or you were charged twice, we refund in full. Otherwise, contact us within 7 days of buying and we'll work it out with you in good faith.

3. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Banks and payment providers usually take 5 to 7 working days to show them.

4. Cancelling

You can stop using Mailzen at any time and ask us to delete your account. Unused credits are not paid out when an account is deleted, except as described above.
