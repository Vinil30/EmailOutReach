This policy explains what data Mailzen (“Mailzen”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use the website and app at this address, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It applies to every account type: students and job seekers, and people running email campaigns.

The short version

  • We use your data only to run Mailzen for you. We never sell it and never use it for advertising.
  • Gmail access is limited to sending email you start. Mailzen cannot read, search or delete anything in your inbox.
  • Some features send recipient and company details to AI and search providers to write emails.
  • You can disconnect Gmail at any time and ask us to delete your account and data.

This summary is for convenience. The full text below is what applies.

1. Who we are

Mailzen is an email outreach tool operated from India. For any privacy question or request, email rapellysaivinil30@gmail.com. We act as the party responsible for your account data. For contact lists you upload, you decide who is on them and why, and we process that data on your instructions (see section 6).

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

  • Account: name, email address, a hashed password (we never store it in readable form) and the account type you pick.
  • Profile: for students, things like your school, skills, goal, resume link, portfolio link, projects and email signature; for campaigners, organisation, website, offer, audience, postal address and opt-out wording.
  • Email content: templates, drafts, subjects and bodies you write or that Mailzen writes for you.
  • Contact lists: names, email addresses, companies and any other columns in files you upload, paste or type in.
  • Signature records: when you agree to our terms we store the name you typed, the document versions and their fingerprints, the time, your IP address and your browser’s user-agent, as proof of agreement.

Data created while you use Mailzen

  • Send history: who was emailed, when, delivery status, spam-check scores and replies counted by your plan.
  • Plan and usage data, such as how many emails you have sent today and in total, and how many database recruiters you have reached.
  • Basic technical logs (IP address, request time, errors) kept by our hosting provider for security and debugging.

Data from Google

If you connect Gmail, Google gives us OAuth tokens for the gmail.send permission and confirms the address you connected. See section 4.

3. How we use data

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To write, spam-check, queue and send the emails you ask for, from your own Gmail account.
  • To enforce plan limits, pace sends 1–3 minutes apart and avoid emailing the same person twice.
  • To show your history, scores and progress.
  • To respond to support requests and keep the service safe (for example, stopping abuse).
  • To keep records needed by law or to prove agreement to our terms.

We do not use your data to train AI models, build advertising profiles or market other products to your contacts.

4. Google user data

Mailzen asks for one Gmail permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. It lets Mailzen send an email from your Gmail account when you start a send. It does not allow reading, searching, labelling or deleting messages, and we never access your inbox or contacts.

We use the tokens only to send emails you have approved or set to send automatically. We store them encrypted in transit and at rest with our database provider, never show them to other users and never transfer them to anyone except as needed to send the email through Google.

Mailzen’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No human at Mailzen reads email content you send through Gmail, except when you ask us for help with a specific message, when needed for security, or when the law requires it.

You can disconnect Gmail from your profile at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens.

6. Contacts and recipients

If you upload a list, you are responsible for having a lawful reason to email the people on it (our Terms explain this). We use list data only to send your emails and keep your history, and we never contact your recipients for our own purposes.

Mailzen also keeps a database of professional recruiter contacts, gathered from public, business-facing sources, that some accounts can email through automation. Account holders never see or download this database. If you are a recipient and want to stop receiving emails sent through Mailzen, or want your details removed from our recruiter database, email us. We will deactivate your details in the recruiter database so no automated outreach reaches you again, and pass your request on to anyone who emailed you from their own list.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and history: while your account is open.
  • After you ask us to delete your account: removed within 30 days, except for signature records and minimal send logs we must keep to prove agreement or handle a legal claim (kept up to 3 years).
  • Gmail tokens: deleted as soon as you disconnect.
  • Hosting logs: kept by our provider for a short period, usually under 30 days.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed, connections use HTTPS, and access to the database is restricted. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you, or get a copy of it;
  • correct anything that is wrong (most of it you can edit in your profile);
  • delete your account and data;
  • withdraw consent, for example by disconnecting Gmail;
  • nominate someone to act for you, and complain to a data protection authority.

Email rapellysaivinil30@gmail.com from your account address. We reply within 30 days.

10. Cookies and local storage

Mailzen does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser’s local storage keeps your sign-in session and your light or dark theme. Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to load them.

11. Children

Mailzen is for people aged 18 or older. If you are under 18, you may use it only with the consent of a parent or guardian, who agrees to these documents for you. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; if we learn of it, we delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the version and date above. For important changes, the app will ask you to read and sign the new version before you can keep sending.
